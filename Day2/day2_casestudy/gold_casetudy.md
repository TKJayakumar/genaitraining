## Executive Summary

On **November 11, 2026**, Chennai gold-rate trackers reported different
retail reference prices. One timestamped Chennai market feed listed
**24K gold at approximately ₹14,593 per gram** and **22K gold at
approximately ₹13,367 per gram** at 10:56 IST. Other widely used
trackers showed lower reference quotes, around ₹14,398--₹14,412 per gram
for 24K and ₹13,199--₹13,211 per gram for 22K.

This variation is important: Chennai does not have one universally
applicable retail jewellery rate. The final price depends on the quote
provider, update time, bullion reference, jeweller premium, purity,
making charges, wastage policy and applicable taxes.

The recent Chennai rate series shows a sharp rise around November 4--5,
followed by a correction on November 6 and relatively stable trading
through November 9--11. The market is therefore showing **short-term
consolidation after a volatile move**, rather than a confirmed one-way
trend.

Key observations:

-   22K gold remains the main reference for ordinary jewellery purchases
    in Chennai.
-   24K rates are more relevant for investment-grade coins and bars.
-   Wedding, festival and auspicious-day buying can support local
    jewellery demand.
-   International gold prices, USD/INR, import costs and domestic
    bullion premiums influence Chennai rates.
-   Buyers should compare the complete invoice, not only the advertised
    per-gram rate.

## 4. Factors Affecting Chennai Gold Prices

### 4.1 International gold price

Gold is traded internationally in US dollars per troy ounce. A rise in
the global gold price generally increases the Indian landed cost, while
a fall can reduce domestic reference prices. International trading
sentiment, investor demand and geopolitical risk can cause rapid
movements.

### 4.2 USD/INR exchange rate

India imports a substantial amount of its gold requirement. If the rupee
weakens against the US dollar, imported gold becomes more expensive in
rupee terms, even when the international dollar price is unchanged.

### 4.3 Import duty and government policy

Import duties and other import-related costs influence the domestic
landed price. The World Gold Council's May 2026 India market update
discussed a reported increase in gold import duty from 6% to 15%,
alongside other measures affecting gold imports. Policy changes should
be checked against current official notifications before being used in a
purchase calculation.

### 4.4 Domestic bullion premium

The domestic price can differ from a simple conversion of the
international spot price. Import availability, local inventory,
logistics, financing and demand can affect the premium or discount in
the Indian market.

### 4.5 Wedding and festival demand

Chennai jewellery demand is influenced by wedding seasons and auspicious
buying occasions. Demand can increase around festivals and wedding
dates, but high prices may encourage consumers to purchase lighter
jewellery or postpone purchases.

### 4.6 Interest rates and inflation

Gold does not pay interest. When interest rates and bond yields rise,
some investors may prefer interest-bearing assets. When inflation,
currency concerns or uncertainty increase, gold may attract safe-haven
and wealth-preservation demand.

### 4.7 International investment flows

Gold-backed ETFs, central-bank purchases and investment demand can
influence global prices. These factors affect Chennai indirectly through
the international benchmark and domestic import cost.

## 5. Jewellery Purchase Cost --- Chennai Example

The gold rate displayed on a website is not the final amount payable for
jewellery.

### Illustrative 22K jewellery calculation

Assume:

-   Gold weight: 10 grams
-   22K reference rate: ₹13,367 per gram
-   Making charge: 10% of gold value
-   GST: 3% on the applicable transaction value

  Component                Calculation                              Amount
  ------------------------ ---------------------------- ------------------
  Gold value               10 × ₹13,367                          ₹1,33,670
  Making charge            10% of gold value                       ₹13,367
  Subtotal                 Gold value + making charge            ₹1,47,037
  GST                      3% of subtotal                        ₹4,411.11
  **Illustrative total**   Subtotal + GST                 **₹1,51,448.11**

*This is a mathematical example, not a jeweller quotation. Actual making
charges, wastage, stone value, discounts and invoice treatment vary by
seller and design. Confirm the applicable GST and invoice breakdown with
the jeweller.*

### Before purchasing jewellery

-   Check the day's 22K rate at the showroom.
-   Confirm the net gold weight separately from stones and other
    materials.
-   Ask whether wastage is charged and how it is calculated.
-   Ask for the making-charge percentage or fixed amount.
-   Verify the final GST amount on the invoice.
-   Check the BIS hallmark and HUID details.
-   Compare the final bill across jewellers for the same purity and
    approximate design.
